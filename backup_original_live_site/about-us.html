Who We Are

"We Treat Food as an Object of Fine Art"

✦

Shree Bhagwati Caterers was founded on a singular Marwari principle: "Atithi Devo Bhava" — Guest is God. Over 20 years, we have grown from an intimate outdoor catering service in Jodhpur into one of Rajasthan's most trusted luxury catering houses.

Through constant innovation, endless culinary endeavors, and an unmatched infrastructure with modern kitchen equipment and traditional brass ware, we have elevated catering into a celebrated art form.

Whether it is a multi-day destination wedding banquet for 3,000 guests at a heritage fort or an intimate Griha Pravesh feast for 50 family members, we maintain zero compromises on freshness, pure desi ghee, and heartfelt warmth.

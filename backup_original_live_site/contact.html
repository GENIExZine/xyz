How many guests can Bhagwati Caterers serve?
We cater for intimate celebrations of 50 guests up to royal wedding banquets of 5,000+ attendees with seamless kitchen and staffing execution.
Do you cater destination weddings outside Jodhpur?
Yes, our master chefs and mobile industrial kitchen setups travel across Jaipur, Udaipur, Jaisalmer, Pushkar, Kumbhalgarh, and throughout India.
Are Jain and pure vegetarian options strictly maintained?
Yes! 100% Pure Vegetarian catering is our heritage, and we provide dedicated separate vessels and culinary cooks for strict Jain (root vegetable-free) feasts.

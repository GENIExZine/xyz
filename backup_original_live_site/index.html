Our Sacred Commitment

Purity, Hygiene & Quality Standards

✦

We treat food preparation with the highest sanctity. From 100% RO mineral water to certified ingredient sourcing, our protocols guarantee total peace of mind.

100% RO Mineral Water

Every beverage, ice cube, curry broth, and cleaning cycle exclusively uses multi-stage RO purified mineral water.

Daily Utensil Sanitization

All cooking equipment, royal brass chafers, tandoors, and serving dishes undergo strict thermal sanitization before every event.

100% Pure Desi Ghee

Our traditional sweets and gravies are prepared strictly in certified pure desi ghee and cold-pressed authentic oils.

Uniformed & Screened Staff

Mandatory chef coats, sanitary gloves, hairnets, and periodic health screenings for all kitchen and service staff.

Temperature-Controlled Prep

Dedicated insulated hot boxes and mobile cold storage preserve food temperatures and peak flavors throughout the banquet.

Dedicated Jain Kitchen Crew

Separate vessels, utensils, and dedicated culinary teams ensure 100% adherence to strict Jain dietary guidelines.
